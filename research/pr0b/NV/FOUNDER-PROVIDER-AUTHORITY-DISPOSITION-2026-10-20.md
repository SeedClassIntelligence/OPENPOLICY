## Founder authorization

> I approve the two Nevada provider-authority rules stated above for verification and local publication testing. Do not deploy or activate production.

## Verified propositions

1. For a current Nevada personal-auto transaction, the producer organization and authenticated natural-person producer must hold applicable active authority. An agent must have an active appointment with the insurer. A broker does not require appointment but must place through a licensed agent of an authorized insurer.
2. A direct carrier transacting Nevada personal-auto insurance must hold current verified Nevada certificate-of-authority evidence.
