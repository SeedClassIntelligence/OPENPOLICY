# Open Polici — Founder Verification Authority

Date: October 20, 2026  
Scope: Nevada Wave 0 regulatory RulePack governance

## Founder direction

The Open Polici founder is the human decision-maker for the product and may perform the human verification and publication role for Nevada rules prepared by Codex. No outside reviewer, outside consultant, separate company or unidentified “independent human” is inherently required by the Open Polici governance model.

The separation-of-duties control is:

1. Codex captures sources, authors candidate propositions, implements the founder's recorded decisions and runs deterministic tests.
2. The founder reviews the prepared evidence and records the legal/product disposition.
3. The founder may publish the resulting ruleset or designate another human publisher.

Codex must not fabricate a founder disposition. Conversely, Codex must not convert this control into an invented requirement that the founder locate an outside person.

## Implementation consequence

- References to an “independent verifier” mean independent from the authoring agent, not independent from Open Polici.
- Founder decisions are attributable human decisions and satisfy that authorship separation.
- Provider-marketplace work must proceed through the prepared evidence and founder disposition workflow rather than remaining indefinitely blocked on a hypothetical third party.
- Production enforcement still consumes only a durably recorded, fully verified and published ruleset.
